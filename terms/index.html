2. What Jyotim offers

Jyotim is a guided devotional practice app. It provides:

  • Audio-guided pujas led by a virtual priest, with visual storytelling
  • Mantra jaap and paath (recitation) practice, with read-along text
  • A personal Vedic astrology (kundli) report and an upaay journey, based on birth details you provide
  • Meera, an AI astrologer you can ask about your kundli, paid by a monthly plan or question packs (Section 8.7)

The pujas, mantra jaap, paath, the kundli report and reminders are free to use, supported by advertising. Meera's chat is paid.

The app is a devotional and cultural aid. It does not perform a puja on your behalf, and it does not replace a priest, a temple, or personal religious guidance.

8.7 Meera, the AI jyotishi: plans and daily limits

Meera is an AI (artificial intelligence) astrologer inside Jyotim. Her answers are generated by a computer program from your Kundli; no person writes them. At launch she answers questions about marriage (Vivah) and business (Vyapar). Her answers are guidance, on the same terms as Section 4. Please do not share another person's personal details in the chat unless you have their permission. You are responsible for what you share.

There are two ways to pay for Meera's questions:

  • Monthly plan. It starts with a 3-day trial for ₹1, with up to 2 questions a day. The trial is offered once per phone number and once per phone. After the trial, ₹199 a month by autopay, with up to 20 questions a day. A question day runs from 4 AM to 4 AM, India time. On the monthly plan, a question you send after the day's limit is reached is kept and sent to Meera after the limit resets, when you next open the chat. During the trial, it waits in your message box for you to send the next day; on the trial's last day, it is kept and sent once your monthly plan starts.
  • Question packs. One-time purchases: 5 questions for ₹25, 10 for ₹39, or 100 for ₹299. Packs are for anyone not on the trial or in a paid month: for example after the trial's autopay is cancelled or paused, when a ₹199 cannot be collected, or when your phone number or your phone has already had a trial. Questions from a pack have no daily limit. Questions from a pack can be used for one year from the date of purchase; questions not used within that year expire.

When a question counts. A question counts only when Meera's answer reaches you. Nothing is counted while you are offline or while Meera is unavailable. If your message is unclear and Meera replies with two or three readings for you to choose from, that reply counts as a question. So that Meera keeps working for everyone, she may ask you to wait a little if many questions are sent in a short time.

Autopay. You will receive a notice at least 24 hours before each ₹199 debit, from your UPI app or bank. If a ₹199 cannot be collected when it is due, the monthly plan does not start, or does not continue, until it is collected, and you may buy question packs in the meantime. We may try to collect it again on the 1st, 3rd and 7th of the month, for up to 3 months; after that, the autopay ends. If you buy a question pack while your autopay is set up, we cancel the ₹199 autopay, so you are never charged for both, and if a ₹199 is collected after you bought a pack, we refund it. If you cancel or pause autopay during the trial, the trial ends at once, and turning autopay back on does not restore it. If you paused it and turn it back on before the first ₹199 is due, that ₹199 is collected and your monthly plan starts. If you turn autopay off during a paid month, you can keep asking until that month ends. To turn autopay off, use your UPI app or your bank, or write to us at support@jyotim.com and ask us to cancel it.

Deleting your account. Deleting your account does not turn off Meera's autopay, and the ₹199 is still collected each month while it is on, even though you can no longer use the chat. To stop it, cancel the autopay before you delete your account, by turning it off in your UPI app or your bank, or by writing to us at support@jyotim.com and asking us to cancel it. You can do either at any time. If you sign in again with the same number while your plan is running, it comes back. Your chats are deleted, and unused pack questions are deleted with the account and are not refunded.
