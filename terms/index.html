2. What Jyotim offers

Jyotim is a guided devotional practice app. It provides:

  • Audio-guided pujas led by a virtual priest, with visual storytelling
  • Mantra jaap and paath (recitation) practice, with read-along text
  • A personal Vedic astrology (kundali) report and an upaay plan, based on birth details you provide — this is part of Jyotim Select, our paid subscription (Section 8). A free preview shows how many findings your kundali has before you decide to subscribe.

The pujas, mantra jaap, paath and reminders are free to use, supported by advertising. The kundali report and the ad-free experience are what the subscription adds.

The app is a devotional and cultural aid. It does not perform a puja on your behalf, and it does not replace a priest, a temple, or personal religious guidance.
