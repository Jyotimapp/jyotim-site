Privacy Policy — Jyotim

Jyotim ("we", "us") is a devotional app that guides you through pujas, mantra and paath practice, and — if you choose — prepares a personal Vedic astrology (kundali) reading.

This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have.

1. The short version

Your birth details never leave your phone.

The birth date, time and place you enter for your kundali, the reading itself, and the puja dedications you set are stored only on your own device, in encrypted form. We do not upload them, and none of our servers hold them. We cannot read them, and neither can anyone who obtains our systems, because we never receive them in the first place.

What does leave your phone: your phone number when you sign in, anything you choose to fill into your profile, and usage and crash data, tied to your account. Your profile is entirely optional — leave it blank and every part of the app still works. None of it carries your birth details.

2. What we collect, and where it goes

2.1 Stays on your device — never transmitted

WhatWhy we ask for it
Birth date, birth time, birth placeTo calculate your kundali and personalise remedies. The calculation runs on your phone, not on a server.
Your relationship to the person a puja is for — for example, your husband or your daughterSo the priest's voice can dedicate the puja to them. We ask for the relationship only, never their name.
Your practice history, streaks, reminders, and puja progressTo resume where you left off and to send the reminders you asked for.

All of the above is encrypted on your device, and is not included in device backups in readable form.

What leavesWhy

That is the whole list. Your birth details, your kundali and your puja dedications are not on it — they stay on your phone (§2.1).

Your usage and crash data are linked to your account, so we can see how one person's experience of the app actually goes rather than only totals. That account is a random number, not your name or your phone number — and the events themselves never carry your name, your birth details or your astrological readings. The app blocks such data automatically before anything is sent, rather than relying on us to remember.

Some of this is handled by service providers acting on our instructions, and some of it is processed on servers outside India, which Indian law permits.

As with any internet request, whoever serves the puja content necessarily sees your device IP address and which files were requested. We do not use this to build a picture of you.

2.3 Advertising and payments

The free version of the app is supported by ads. Our advertising provider uses your device's advertising identifier — a resettable ID that Android provides, not your name and not your birth details — to show ads, to limit how often the same ad repeats, and to show ads chosen for you based on your activity across apps (personalised ads). Where the law requires your consent first, the app asks before doing so. You can switch personalised ads off at any time in the app's Settings — you still see ads, just not personalised ones — and you can reset the identifier or switch them off for your whole device in Settings → Google → Ads. Subscribers to Jyotim Select are shown no ads at all.

Your birth details and your kundali are never used for advertising, and are never shared with any advertising provider. They cannot be — they never leave your device.

When you subscribe, the payment is handled by the provider you pay through: Google Play, or our payment partner for UPI AutoPay, cards and net banking. They receive your payment details; we do not. What reaches us is only whether a subscription is active, which plan it is, and when it renews — never your card number, UPI ID, or bank credentials.

3. Signing in with your phone number

To use Jyotim you sign in with your mobile number. We send a one-time code to that number by SMS; entering the code signs you in.

What we store. Your phone number, on our servers in India, linked to a random account identifier — together with whatever you have chosen to put in your profile. You can change or clear any profile field at any time in the app. Everything else in the app — and our analytics — uses that identifier, never the number itself. Your number is not sent to our analytics or advertising providers, and is not shared with anyone else.

Why we ask for it. To sign you in and keep your progress if you change phones, and to make sure each introductory offer is taken once per person. We do not send you marketing messages on it without asking you first.

Who else sees it. The SMS carrying your code is delivered by a messaging provider acting on our instructions; it receives your number only to send that message.

How long we keep it. For as long as your account exists. Deleting your account (Section 6) deletes it — apart from the coded marker described in Section 5.

4. Why we are allowed to process this (legal basis)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), we process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give by entering the details yourself — your phone number when you sign in, your birth details when you ask for a kundali. You are told what each field is for at the point where you enter it. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data (Section 6 below) or by uninstalling the app.

5. How long we keep it

Because your birth details and profile live on your own device, you control their retention. They are kept until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app.

Your phone number, your profile and your account are kept for as long as your account exists, and deleted when you delete your account.

One coded marker is kept after deletion. An introductory offer can be taken once per person, so that a deleted account cannot claim it a second time we keep a coded record that the offer was used. It cannot be turned back into your phone number, it holds nothing else about you, it is never shared, and it is used for nothing else.

Usage and crash data are retained by our providers under their own retention schedules — currently up to 12 months for usage events and up to 90 days for crash reports. When you delete your account, we permanently remove the link between you and that data, so whatever is still held cannot be traced back to you.

6. Your rights, and how to actually use them

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to access, correct, and erase your personal data, to grievance redressal, and to nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf.

  • See or correct your data: your name and email are in the app under Profile → Personal Details, and your birth details can be corrected from the kundali screen. Changing them there updates them everywhere we hold them.
  • Delete your account and data: open the app → Settings → Mera data delete karein. This deletes your account, your phone number and your profile from our systems, and permanently unlinks your usage and crash data so it can no longer be connected to you. On your phone it removes your birth details, profile, sankalp, practice history and reminders, cancels any scheduled reminders, and destroys the encryption key so that anything remaining is permanently unreadable. It cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything held on your phone — but it does not delete your account. To delete the account itself, use Delete my data before uninstalling, or write to support@jyotim.com afterwards (the steps are at jyotim.com/delete-account).
  • Usage and crash data are linked to your account for as long as it exists. When you delete your account, we permanently delete that link. What remains cannot be connected back to you — not by us, and not by anyone else — and survives only inside overall totals, in the way a completed survey response does. You can ask us about your data at any time at support@jyotim.com.

7. Children

Jyotim is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and we ask you to confirm this when you sign in. Under India's DPDP Act anyone under 18 is a child, and processing a child's personal data requires verifiable parental consent — so rather than collect it, we decline: the app is not offered to anyone under 18.

The kundali reading checks again. It is the one part of the app that asks for a birth date, and if the date entered shows the person is under 18, the reading is not generated and the details are not saved.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at support@jyotim.com and we will delete it.

8. Security

Personal data on your device is encrypted.

Your phone number and your profile are held on our servers in India, restricted so that only the sign-in system can read them, and never written into logs or analytics.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. What we can say is that we hold as little as possible: your birth details and your kundali never reach us at all, so no breach of ours can expose them.

If we change what we collect or how we use it — for example if online accounts are introduced, or if we add a new provider — we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above, and where the change is significant we will tell you inside the app.
