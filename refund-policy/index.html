Refund and Cancellation Policy

This page explains what Jyotim costs, how to cancel, and when we refund. It summarises Section 8 of our Terms of Service; if the two ever differ, the Terms apply.

1. What is free, and what is paid

Jyotim is free to download. Guided pujas, mantra jaap, paath and shubh tithi reminders can be used without paying; the free version is supported by advertising.

Jyotim Select is our paid subscription. It removes advertising and unlocks the detailed kundali report and its upaay plan. Everything is digital and delivered inside the app — we sell no physical goods, so nothing is shipped.

The purchase screen always shows what you pay today, what you will be charged next, and on which date — the price shown when you subscribe is the price that applies.

You can pay through Google Play, or through our payment partner with UPI AutoPay or a card mandate.

3. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, and cancelling stops all future charges.

  • Paid through Google Play: cancel in the Play Store under Subscriptions.
  • Paid through UPI AutoPay or a card mandate: cancel in the app under Settings. You can also remove the mandate itself in your bank or UPI app, under AutoPay or mandates.

During a paid quarter or week: you keep Jyotim Select until the end of the period you have already paid for. No refund is given for the current period, and nothing further is charged.

During the 1-day trial: cancel within the trial day and nothing more is charged. The paid features end straight away, and the ₹1 trial charge is not refunded.

4. Refunds

Paid through Google Play: refunds follow Google Play's refund policy, and requests go to Google.

Paid through our payment partner: if you were charged in error — for example a duplicate debit, a renewal after you cancelled, or a payment that went through when the app failed to deliver what you paid for — write to support@jyotim.com within 7 days of the charge, with the payment reference. Where the claim is correct, we refund to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval.

Apart from these cases, a billing period that has started is not refunded.

5. Contact

Write to support@jyotim.com with your payment reference for anything about a charge, a cancellation or a refund.
