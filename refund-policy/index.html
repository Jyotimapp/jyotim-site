Refund and Cancellation Policy

This page explains what Jyotim costs, how to cancel, and when we refund. It summarises Section 8 of our Terms of Service; if the two ever differ, the Terms apply.

1. What is free, and what is paid

Jyotim is free to download. Guided pujas, mantra jaap, paath, your Kundli report and upaay journey and shubh tithi reminders can be used without paying; the free version is supported by advertising.

Meera's chat is paid. Meera is the AI astrologer inside Jyotim; you pay for her answers with a monthly plan or with question packs. Neither removes advertising. Everything is digital and delivered inside the app — we sell no physical goods, so nothing is shipped.

3. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, and cancelling stops all future charges.

Meera's monthly plan: turn off Jyotim's AutoPay in your UPI app or with your bank, or write to us at support@jyotim.com and ask us to cancel it.

Deleting your account does not cancel Meera's AutoPay: the ₹199 is still collected each month while it is on. Cancel it first in one of the ways above. Unused pack questions are deleted with the account and are not refunded.

During the trial: if you cancel or pause the AutoPay, the trial ends at once, there is no second trial, and the ₹1 is not refunded. The ₹199 is then not collected, unless you paused the AutoPay and turn it back on before the ₹199 is due; in that case it is collected and the monthly plan starts. If you do nothing, the first ₹199 is collected after the 3-day trial ends, never before, and you are notified at least 24 hours before it is debited.

During a paid month: you can keep asking until the end of the month you have paid for. No refund is given for the current month, and nothing further is charged.

Question packs are one-time purchases, so there is nothing to cancel.

If a ₹199 payment fails, we may try it again on the 1st, 3rd and 7th of the month, for up to 3 months, and then the AutoPay ends. Until a ₹199 goes through, you can buy question packs. If you buy a pack while the AutoPay is set up, we cancel the AutoPay so you are never charged for both. We never retry at a different price.

Jyotim Select:

  • Paid through Google Play: cancel in the Play Store under Subscriptions.
  • Paid through UPI AutoPay or a card mandate: cancel in the app under Help & Support → FAQ → "Select cancel kaise karein?". This cancels the subscription and stops the mandate.

4. Refunds

Paid through Google Play: refunds follow Google Play's refund policy, and requests go to Google.

Paid through our payment partner: if you were charged in error — for example a duplicate debit, a renewal after you cancelled, or a payment that went through when the app failed to deliver what you paid for — write to support@jyotim.com within 7 days of the charge, with the payment reference. Where the claim is correct, we refund to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval.

Refunded automatically, without your asking: a ₹1 trial payment from a phone number or phone that has already had a trial; a ₹199 collected after you bought a question pack in its place; and a question pack bought while your trial or a paid month is still running.

Apart from these cases, a billing period that has started is not refunded. The ₹1 trial is not refunded when you cancel it. A question pack is not refunded once its questions are added to your account, including questions left unused when they expire or when you delete your account.

5. Contact

Write to support@jyotim.com with your payment reference for anything about a charge, a cancellation or a refund. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 7 working days.
